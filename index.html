About

I'm the independent developer behind GeoSort. I build Android apps that help people organize the photos and screenshots already on their phone — by where a photo was taken, and by which app a screenshot came from.

I care about practical tools and straightforward interfaces. No accounts required. No GeoSort server. Your media stays on your device.

GeoSort

GeoSort is an Android app for organizing camera photos and videos into folders based on where they were taken. You draw geographic bubbles on a map, link each one to a folder, and sort from there. It also includes a Screenshots tab that groups screenshots by the app that captured them, so you can browse and clean them up without digging through a single camera roll.
